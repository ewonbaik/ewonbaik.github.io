Ewon Baik

Welcome! I am a PhD Candidate in Political Science and International Relations at the University of Southern California. I am currently on the academic job market.

A running theme in my research is how immigration, institutions, and international relations inform individuals’ political behavior and states’ domestic politics, particularly among the people from and the states of East Asia. My work has been funded by the USC Graduate School Dean’s Dissertation Fellowship, Center for International Studies, East Asian Studies Center, Dornsife College, and the Bartling Endowed Fellowship.

I received my MA in Political Science from Seoul National University and a BA in Politics from Princeton University.
